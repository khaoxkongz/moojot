# MooJot illustration prompts

These illustrations were regenerated with imagegen for the warm charcoal, cream, and terracotta app theme. The image at `brand-mascot.png` was used as the character reference for the rest of the set. The previous asset was also supplied as a composition reference for each replacement image; its earlier blue and yellow colors and flat drawing style were not retained.

## Shared visual brief

> A small, friendly pig mascot for a Thai personal finance app, with a rounded snout, soft ears, expressive dot eyes, and a gentle smile. Render as a warm, tactile picture book illustration with subtle paper and plush texture, soft edges, muted terracotta and peach on cream, warm charcoal details, and restrained carrot orange and leaf green accents. Preserve the scene's purpose and mobile readability. Transparent PNG cutout with real alpha outside the subjects; no rectangular backdrop, lettering, numerals, logo, cat, or watermark. Maintain the same pig's facial proportions, texture, and palette as the supplied brand mascot.
